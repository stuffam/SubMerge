SubMerge v1.1.4
===============

Settings and Key Bindings now open the standard Sublime way. Nothing
about how SubMerge compares files has changed.


WHAT CHANGED
------------

SubMerge opened its settings through a command of its own. It worked, but
it meant SubMerge sat outside the convention every other package follows,
and tooling that goes looking for a package's settings the standard way
could not find them.

Both entries now call Sublime's own "edit_settings", so they behave
exactly like Preferences > Settings does for any other package.


ON THE COMMAND PALETTE
----------------------

Two entries have been renamed to match the convention:

  was  "SubMerge: Settings"        now  "Preferences: SubMerge Settings"
  was  "SubMerge: Key Bindings"    now  "Preferences: SubMerge Key Bindings"

They now sort alongside every other package's preferences. Typing
"submerge" in the palette still finds both, so nothing is harder to reach.

Every other palette entry is unchanged.


THE MENUS ARE WHERE THEY WERE
-----------------------------

  Tools > SubMerge > Settings
  Tools > SubMerge > Key Bindings
  Preferences > Package Settings > SubMerge

Same captions, same places. Both still open in split view, with the
documented defaults on the left and your own overrides on the right.

Your existing settings and key bindings are untouched.


No need to restart for this update.
