SubMerge v1.1.4
===============

Settings and Key Bindings now open the standard Sublime way, so they
behave like every other package's.

On the Command Palette they are now "Preferences: SubMerge Settings" and
"Preferences: SubMerge Key Bindings". The menus are unchanged.

Full notes: https://github.com/stuffam/SubMerge/releases/tag/v1.1.4
