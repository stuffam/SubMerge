SubMerge v1.1.2
===============

Everything SubMerge does is now on the Command Palette.


THE COMMAND PALETTE
-------------------

Press Ctrl+Shift+P (Cmd+Shift+P on macOS) and type "submerge". Every
command is there — starting a comparison, moving between differences,
copying changes, the comparison options, the color presets, settings and
the user guide.

Previously the only ways in were the Tools > SubMerge menu, the
right-click menus and the keyboard shortcuts. If you drive Sublime mostly
from the palette, SubMerge was close to invisible.

Nothing has moved. The menus and shortcuts are exactly where they were —
this is an additional way in, not a replacement.

Every entry is prefixed "SubMerge:", so typing "submerge" brings up the
whole list and nothing else.


ALSO
----

The two internal commands that rewrite buffer contents stay out of the
palette, as they always have. The test suite now checks both that every
palette entry points at a command that actually exists, and that those
two internal ones are never listed.

No settings, shortcuts or behavior have changed, and there is no need to
restart for this update.
